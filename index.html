HOW TO PLAY

  1. Hunt. Drag below your castle to pull back, then release. Every arrow you own is loosed at once, side by side down that line. An arrow bounces off the field edge and is spent on the first animal it hits.
  2. Units move. Each squad strikes what is in reach, steps forward if the way is clear, and strikes again where it stops.
  3. Spend. Each turn deals you 4 different cards. Drag them onto the board; a played card is replaced by a new one at once.
  • Prey. The number over an animal is its meat and its health. Each arrow takes its DMG off that number and you bank the same amount. Quick animals (gold number) keep to the middle of the field and give up everything to one hit, but you have to lead them. Bigger prey arrives every 5 turns.
  • +1 Arrow / +Damage go on your castle. More arrows per hunt, or more meat from each arrow. Each gets dearer every time.
  • Warriors are the all-round fighters.
  • Archers shoot 2 slots ahead, but die to a single blow from anything.
  • Giants fight like warriors but are slower and dearer. Their job is towers: 2 blows.
  • A column sorts itself as it marches: giants to the front, warriors next, archers at the back. A squad moving up swaps places with the lower ones in its way.
  • Tower sits on a checkpoint you hold and blocks the road. Without a giant it takes warriors and archers 6 blows to break.
  • Troops go on either road, from your gate up to the furthest checkpoint your units hold. They step forward as they land and strike if that puts an enemy in reach.

Break the red castle to win. If both castles stand after 25 turns, the star decides: your castle's health plus 10 for every checkpoint you hold.
