HOW TO PLAY

  1. Hunt. Drag anywhere to aim (pull back like a slingshot) and release one arrow. It bounces 5 times and takes meat from every animal it hits.
  2. Units move. Your troops march a step and strike whatever they reach, towers shoot, hunters walk.
  3. Attack. Drag cards onto the board to spend meat. You get 4 different cards a turn and can play each once.
  • Warriors hit hard up close and walk past your archers to meet the enemy.
  • Archers stop at range and shoot. They are fragile, so send them behind warriors.
  • Troops go on either road, from your gate up to the furthest checkpoint your units hold. Lose the units and you lose the checkpoints.
  • Guard Tower sits on a checkpoint you hold. Weak, but the enemy must break it to pass.
  • Hunter Tower goes in your part of the field and shoots animals that come close.
  • Hunter walks up the field. You can build as far forward as your furthest hunter.

Break the red castle to win. Castles shoot troops at their gate, so keep the waves coming. After 25 turns the healthier castle wins.
