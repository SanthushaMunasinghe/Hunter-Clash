HOW TO PLAY

  1. Hunt. Press in the strip below your castle and slide left or right to aim (the shot swings the opposite way), then let go. Every arrow you own is loosed at once, side by side down that line. An arrow bounces off the field edge and stops in the first animal it hits, unless it kills it with damage to spare: then it flies on through with what is left.
  2. Castle defends. After your hunt your castle fires 5 arrows down each road at the enemy's front row, if it has come close to your gate. One arrow kills a warrior or an archer.
  3. Spend. Each turn deals you 4 different cards; a played card is replaced by a new one at once. Drag a troop card onto the left or right side of the board to send it down that road.
  4. End turn. Nothing fights until you press END TURN. Then your front row strikes, the archers behind it shoot if they are in range, and your columns march up to one checkpoint further.
  • Prey. The number over an animal is its meat and its health. Each arrow takes its DMG off that number and you bank the same amount. Quick animals (gold number) keep to the middle of the field and give up everything to one hit, but you have to lead them. Bigger prey arrives every 5 turns.
  • +1 Arrow / +Damage go on your castle. More arrows per hunt (3 at most), or more meat from each arrow; +Damage gets dearer each time.
  • Troops step out of your gate and walk up to join your column on that road. They stand in rows of up to 5: warriors and giants together, archers in rows of their own. A row fills up before a new one starts behind, melee always take the front row, and rows close up as units fall.
  • Warriors come 3 to a card. Two blows fell a warrior.
  • Archers come 3 to a card and die to any blow, but shoot 3 slots ahead, so they fight from the rows behind.
  • Giants come one to a card and stand in the middle of a row, taking 2 spaces. A giant has the health and punch of three warriors and breaks a tower in 2 blows.
  • Tower sits on a checkpoint you hold and blocks the road. It shoots one arrow a turn at the enemy's front row.
  • The first row on an empty road forms up on your nearest checkpoint. When a column has no room left behind it, new units push it forward, unless the enemy is in the way: then that road is full.

Break the red castle to win. If both castles stand after 25 turns, the star decides: your castle's health plus 10 for every checkpoint you hold.
