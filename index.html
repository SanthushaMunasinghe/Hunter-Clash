HOW TO PLAY

  1. Hunt. Drag to aim, release to fire one arrow. It bounces 5 times and takes meat from every animal it hits. A clear shot can hit the red castle.
  2. Units move. After your shot, your troops and hunters each attack or step forward.
  3. Attack. Spend meat by dragging cards onto the board, then end your turn.
  • Warriors / Archers go on the left or right road, anywhere up to your forward-most checkpoint. Archers shoot 3 steps but are fragile, so send them behind warriors.
  • Hunter goes in your part of the field. It shoots the closest animal ahead, moves up after a kill, and fights enemy hunters and the castle.
  • Wall goes in your part of the field and blocks enemy arrows and hunters.
  • Guard Tower goes on a checkpoint you hold. Your troops pass; enemies must break it.

Destroy the red castle to win. From turn 12, castles take double damage.
